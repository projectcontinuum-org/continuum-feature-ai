continuum-feature-ai has been deployed.

This chart is a standalone feature worker. It expects continuum-infra and
continuum-platform to already be installed in the same namespace.

If continuum-infra was installed under a release name other than
"continuum-infra", override continuum.infra.* to match its actual service
names.

If continuum-platform was installed under a release name other than
"continuum-platform", override:
  - continuum.secrets.existingMinioSecret (expected: "<platform-release>-minio-secret")
  - continuum.apiServer.host (expected: "<platform-release>-api-server")

To verify the deployment:

  kubectl get pods -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} -l app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }}
